THE PROBLEM

Running a challenge by hand doesn't scale past a handful of people.

Most coaches manually send daily messages, chase check-ins, track who's falling behind, and reconcile payments — every single day of every cohort.

It caps how many people you can run at once, and every missed follow-up looks unprofessional to the person who paid you.

FEATURES

Everything a real program needs.

No-Code Content Management

Every daily message, onboarding text, and payment message lives in PocketBase and is edited live — no redeploy, no restart.

Smart Scheduler

Messages sent at the correct local time for every user, individually staggered to handle large single-timezone cohorts.

Buddy Accountability

Users pair up, exchange reports, and earn XP for each other — the mechanic that drives completion.

Telegram Stars Payments

Native in-app payments with automatic access control — no external payment links, no manual reconciliation.

Powerful Admin Panel

Broadcast, stats, user management, SQL console, and scheduler control — directly from Telegram.

Gamification

XP, streaks, progress tracking, and leaderboards that keep participants coming back each day.

Completion Exam & Certificate

Optional graded exam after course completion — configurable time limit, passing score, and question bank — with an auto-generated certificate.

Content Protection

Daily content is sent with Telegram's forward/save protection enabled by default, deterring casual resharing.

Robust Infrastructure

Redis caching, Circuit Breaker, rate limiting, and idempotency guards against duplicate sends.

QUESTIONS

Before you ask.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Day-to-day changes — text, schedule, daily content — go through the Web Settings Panel (Standard and Premium) or PocketBase's built-in UI (Basic). A developer is only needed for bigger custom features.

Is this a subscription?

No. It's a one-time payment per package — there's no recurring license fee to keep the bot running.

What if I need help after launch?

Every package includes 30 days of free support and bug fixes, plus documentation and handover materials, so you're not left on your own right after delivery.

Do I fully own the code?

You own everything specific to your bot — content, branding, configuration, and custom features built for your program. The underlying reusable framework stays licensed to you for running your own bot rather than transferred outright, so it can keep being maintained and improved across all clients. Full terms are in the EULA that comes with your proposal.

Can this be customized for my specific program?

Yes. Premium includes custom branding and program-specific logic, and requirements outside any package (multi-language, custom integrations, a different number of days) get a custom quote — just describe what you need.

Where does it actually run?

On your own Railway account, with your own PostgreSQL and Redis instances — not shared infrastructure you're renting access to.
